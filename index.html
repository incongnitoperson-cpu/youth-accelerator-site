01The model

Three pillars, one goal.

We don't hand students a generic playbook and walk away. We provide the three things early founders most consistently lack.

01 — FUNDING
Funding
A R&D microgrant is often all that stands between a prototype and a dead idea. We make sure cost isn't the reason a project never ships.
02 — NETWORKING
Networking
We use our mentor-matching algorithm to match founders with experienced mentors and a network of operators, advisors, and clients. These are the connections and sources of inspiration that capital alone cannot buy.
03 — INCUBATION
Incubation
Help doesn't stop at a check and an intro. Founders draw on an ongoing library of resources for every stage of building.
